Privacy Policy

Orbit uses Roblox OAuth to connect your Roblox account. Orbit does not collect or store your Roblox password.

Information we use

When you authorize Orbit, Roblox may provide your public profile identity and public experiences. This information is used only to display your launcher profile and game library.

Storage and security

Orbit stores a signed session cookie in your browser. OAuth client secrets remain on the server and are never exposed to the browser. You can revoke access from your Roblox account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of this Orbit deployment.
